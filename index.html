Why another goal tracker?

Because every existing one makes the same trade — your data for their features. LivSahaj Goal Tracker makes no such trade.

Accounts that serve the app, not you

Most apps require an account to collect usage data, send marketing emails, and lock your data to their platform. Our account system exists for one reason — your ownership. Your data, your history, your identity. Transferable. Exportable. Yours.

Tools that break when you need them most

Unreliable internet is a fact of life — flights, commutes, remote locations, hospital waiting rooms. A productivity tool that requires a connection isn't a productivity tool. It's a web page. LivSahaj Goal Tracker is installed on your device and works everywhere, always.

Your data on someone else's server

Your goals, your streaks, your daily habits — they reveal patterns about how you think, what you value, and how you live. That data belongs to you. In the free tier, none of it leaves your device. Not because we promise it — because the architecture makes it impossible.

Reports and insights behind a paywall

Many tools give you basic tracking for free, then charge you to understand your own patterns. Our free tier includes the core tracking loop. Reports and cloud sync come with Pro — not because we gatekeep insights, but because those features cost real infrastructure to run honestly.

Built for the long game

Not just a regular habit streak, to-do list, or task manager. A system that makes you accountable for your life — and helps you become the person you have always wanted to be.

🔐
Privacy-first architecture

In the free tier, your data never leaves your device. No server receives it — not because we promise privacy, but because the architecture enforces it. Cloud sync in Pro is opt-in, encrypted, and exists only for your convenience across devices.

📡
Genuinely offline capable

Installed as a PWA, the app lives on your device. Open it on a plane, underground, in a dead zone. Every feature works without a connection. This is not a "limited offline mode" — it is the full application, always.

🧠
Life coach journal

Set your daily intention before the day starts. Reflect on how it actually went. An activity map shows your consistency across months — an honest mirror, not a motivational poster. No algorithm reads it unless you ask the AI coach to.

📊
Weekly and monthly reports

Completion rates, XP trends, category breakdowns. Click any day or week to see exactly what happened. Pattern recognition requires data — this gives you yours, clearly, without interpretation you didn't ask for.

⏱
Focus timer

Countdown timers with quick break presets. Link a session to a specific goal. Speaks the completion out loud. Designed for deep work — not constant interruption, not notification creep.

🎯
Daily goal tracking

Add daily goals by category and priority. Mark done, carry forward, reflect. XP accumulates into a rank system across 16 levels — not gamification for its own sake, but a structured record of who you are becoming over time.
